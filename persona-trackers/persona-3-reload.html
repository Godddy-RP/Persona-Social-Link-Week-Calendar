        Notes

        • Schedules. The grid applies to a normal school day. On Sundays and holidays only all-day links are available (Maya, Akinari, Mamoru, Nozomi, the night ones and a few more). During exams almost no school link appears.
        • Exams. According to one guide: May 18–22, Jul 14–18, Oct 13–16 and Dec 14–18. Mitsuru is still available during the December ones.
        • Rescues. Bunkichi disappears in Tartarus on Sep 12 and must be rescued before Oct 4; Maiko disappears on Oct 21 and must be rescued before Nov 3. Otherwise the links are lost.
        • Arcana. With a Persona of the same Arcana equipped, each meeting gives more points. At R10 of each link you can fuse its Ultimate Persona.
        • Stats. Fuuka requires Courage 6, Yukari Charm 6 and Mitsuru Academics 6, so it pays to raise those stats before their dates. Tanaka, Mutatsu and Mamoru require level 4, and Akinari, Academics 4.
        • Uncertain data. Days: Hidetoshi is listed on Monday, Wednesday and Friday in most guides and Monday to Saturday in others; Mitsuru, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday in most and Monday to Saturday in others; Tanaka, Tuesday and Saturday (one guide adds Thursday and another Monday and Sunday); Mamoru, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday (others add Tuesday or Saturday); Bunkichi and Mitsuko, Tuesday to Saturday or Tuesday to Sunday; Maya is listed on Sundays and holidays only (one guide says every day). Start dates: Fuuka Jun 19 or Jun 22, Yukari Jul 24 or Jul 25, Yuko Apr 24 or Apr 25, Chihiro Apr 27 or May 7, Bebe May 8 or May 29, Mutatsu May 23 or Jul 26, Mamoru Aug 2 or Aug 5, Akinari Aug 9 or Aug 30, Nozomi Apr 28 or May 13, Aigis Jan 8 or Jan 9. Mutatsu requires Courage 4 in two guides and 2 in two others; Aigis is listed on Friday only in one guide.
        • Sources. RPG Site (per-link guides), Game8, Push Square, Inverse, a Steam guide, PlayerAuctions and GameFAQs, cross-checked against each other. If something doesn’t match your playthrough, it may depend on your stats or on what you’ve already done.
