        Notes

        • Weather. The cloud icon marks links you can meet even when it rains: Fox, Yumi/Ayane, Dojima, Nanako and Margaret. Sayoko and Shu get a dimmed cloud because sources disagree. The rest don't appear when it rains.
        • Investigation. While someone is trapped in the TV, your teammates (Yosuke, Yukiko, Chie, Kanji, Rise, Naoto) and Marie aren't available in the afternoon.
        • Exams. According to one guide, Kou/Daisuke and Yumi/Ayane don't appear the week before exams.
        • Arcana. With a Persona of the same Arcana equipped, each meeting gives more points.
        • Jobs. The hospital job (Sayoko) requires Diligence 3 and the tutor job (Shu) requires Understanding 5. The daycare job (Eri) has no stat requirement.
        • Dojima and Nanako. They show up at home in the evening on fixed dates that follow no weekly pattern, and never on the same day. That's why they carry an asterisk every evening. Both close on Oct 31 (one guide says Nov 3 for Dojima); Nanako returns from Jan 23 in Golden.
        • Last day. Except for Adachi (Nov 1), Dojima and Nanako (Oct 31) and Marie (Dec 23), everyone ends between Jan 26 and Feb 5. Those dates come from a single calendar.
        • Uncertain data. Days: Yosuke on Fridays, Yukiko some Sundays, Naoto on Sundays, and for Kou/Daisuke and Yumi/Ayane some guides give other days (Mon/Tue/Wed/Sat/Sun and Mon/Tue/Fri). The start date varies by source: Rise (Jul 23 or Jul 11), Eri (Apr 23 or May 2) and Naoki (Jun 8 or Jun 9). Rain: one guide says Yumi/Ayane don't appear and another says Naoto does. Naoki's Understanding 3 requirement and Fox's Expression 5 at R10 come from a minority of guides. The Fool and Judgement perks are listed incompletely.
        • Sources. RPG Site, Megami Tensei Wiki, Steam guides and CBR, cross-checked against each other. If something doesn't match your game, it may depend on your stats or on what you've already done.
