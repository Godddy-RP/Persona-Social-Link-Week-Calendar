    Story Confidants

    They don’t appear in the weekly grid: they rank up with the story or in the Velvet Room. Under each one is the date on which each rank is reached.

      Social Stats

      Which level of each stat you need, and for which confidant. Set your current level and the requirements you already meet are dimmed.

      Mementos Requests

      The 33 Mementos requests in Royal, with the date each one unlocks. Mark the ones you’ve done and choose how to view them. The first ones have a fixed date; the ones further down open when you rank up a confidant, so they have no date of their own. “Needed for” shows which confidant rank needs that request. “R7.1” is the event that happens right after rank 7.

        Notes

        • Weather. The grid applies to a normal day. The rain icon marks who can be seen when it rains (Ann, Yusuke, Akechi, Iwai, Ohya, Togo and Mishima); Iwai, Togo and Ohya even show up on days that aren’t their usual days. Haru, Futaba, Chihaya and Yoshida don’t appear when it rains. No clear data was found for Ryuji, Makoto, Takemi, Kawakami, Sojiro, Oda, Maruki and Kasumi, so they have no icon.
        • Kasumi *. Wednesdays all year; Thursdays and Sundays only sometimes in July, August and December.
        • Maruki. Until Sep 20, only Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri. The Megami Tensei Wiki adds Thursdays from that date; Prima Games doesn’t.
        • Mishima. He doesn’t appear for 2 or 3 days after each rank up (sources differ).
        • Third Semester. Reaching Maruki rank 9 before Nov 17 unlocks it. That is when rank 11 (Third Awakening) becomes available for Ann, Makoto, Yusuke, Futaba, Haru and Ryuji, along with ranks 6 and up for Kasumi.
        • Uncertain data. Haru: Prima Games and a spreadsheet require Proficiency 5 at R2, while GameFAQs places it at R4. Kawakami: Guts 3 at R1 is listed only on GameFAQs. Akechi R8: one guide says until Nov 20, the rest say Nov 17. The area of each Mementos request comes from Ludo. Perks: Igor has Third Eye at R2 according to Samurai Gamers and at R3 according to GameFAQs; Ryuji’s Insta-kill is listed at R7 in one guide and at R8 in another; the rank for Ryuji (Stealth Dash), Akechi (Sleuthing Mastery), Oda (Warning Shot) and Chihaya comes from a single guide; Akechi also has Follow Up and Smooth Talk listed with no rank. Sae: the R10 perk True Justice has no description in the source. Mementos: one guide puts the requests after Futaba’s Palace one day later (Aug 3, 4 and 5) and another places “Man of Many Faces and Debts” on Nov 10; the date of “If Cats Disappeared from the City” (May 25) comes from a single guide.
        • Story Confidants. Igor, Morgana, Justine and Caroline, and Sae are under Ranks, at the end. Sae has no fixed date from R7 to R9 (she advances with her Palace), and Justine and Caroline rank up with fusion requests, not dates. The World has no ranks or perks: sources list it as a story-only arcana.
        • Sources. Prima Games, RPG Site, GameFAQs, Ludo, The Gamer and Megami Tensei Wiki, cross-checked against each other. If something doesn’t match your playthrough, availability may depend on your stats or pending requests.
